Who we help

We work with adults who are ready to drop stubborn fat, get stronger, and build habits that last—not just in the gym, but in life. Maybe you've tried big-box gyms and felt lost. Maybe you're coming back after a break. Either way, you get a clear plan, real accountability, and coaching that meets you where you are.

Our approach

We focus on long-term progress, not quick fixes. You get a program built for your body and goals, habit accountability so you stay on track, and simple nutrition guidance that fits your life. The result: you don't just change how you look—you build the consistency that makes it stick.

Our model

Start with open gym access at a fair rate—no franchise markup, just a serious place to train. Want more direction and faster results? Add coaching built around your goals. You pick your level; we give you the structure and support to get there.

Results

What you get

  • Better body composition and less stubborn fat
  • Noticeably stronger—in the gym and in daily life
  • More energy and clarity
  • A plan every time you walk in—no more “what do I do today?”
  • Accountability that follows you outside the gym

Training

Personal training

One-on-one programming built for your body, schedule, and goals. You get a plan that fits your life—whether you're after fat loss, muscle gain, or training around an injury—plus the accountability to follow through.

Group training

Structured sessions with a coach in the room and a group that shows up. Stay consistent, stay motivated, and keep the cost down while you build the habit.

Nutrition & accountability

What you do outside the gym decides your results. We help you set simple targets (calories, protein), build habits you can keep, and stay accountable so the progress doesn't stop at the door.

About

I'm Eli, owner of TNT Fitness.

Before TNT, I was a firefighter EMT. That job taught me that discipline, preparation, and showing up for people matter—every day. I brought that same mindset into coaching and into this gym.

I started TNT because I believe training should be more than showing up and sweating. It should be structured. Intentional. And it should make you better—in your body and in the rest of your life. I've seen what happens when people get a clear plan and real accountability: they get results, and they keep them.

My goal here is simple: help you lose fat, build muscle, and build habits you can keep. Whether you're starting from zero or ready to get serious, you get a clear plan and honest coaching from someone who cares about your progress.

This isn't a big box gym. You're not a number. You're known, encouraged, and pushed to improve. Growth doesn't happen by accident—it happens by design.
